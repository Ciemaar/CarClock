# Infiniti QX60 Clock - User Guide

Welcome to your custom dashboard clock, specifically designed for your 2026 Infiniti QX60's native infotainment screen.

This app is designed to look beautiful, act as a helpful utility while driving, and automatically adjust to your car's environment.

## 🕒 Clock Faces

The app comes with two distinct, distraction-free clock faces:

1. **Analog Face (Default)**: A clean, traditional watch face. It features an elegantly sweeping second hand styled in dark Infiniti Green.
2. **Digital Face**: A large, easy-to-read modern format showing the time (e.g., `2:30 PM`).

You also have the option to display the current **Date** directly below the clock face (e.g., `Thursday, January 1`).

---

## 👆 How to Use the App

The clock is designed to be completely distraction-free while driving, meaning there are no complex menus to navigate.

### Opening the Settings Menu

To change your clock preferences:

1. Simply **tap anywhere** on the empty background of the clock screen.
2. The `Clock Settings` menu will slide up into view.

### Changing the Clock Type

1. Open the Settings Menu.
2. Find the row labeled **Clock Type**.
3. Tap the switch to toggle between **Digital** and **Analog**.

### Showing or Hiding the Date

1. Open the Settings Menu.
2. Find the row labeled **Show Date**.
3. Tap the switch to toggle it **On** or **Off**.

### Closing the Settings

When you are done adjusting your preferences, tap the large green **Close** button at the bottom of the menu, or simply tap anywhere in the dark background outside of the menu to instantly return to your clock.

## 🌙 Day & Night Modes (Automatic)

The clock app is seamlessly integrated with your QX60's headlight sensors to prevent dashboard glare at night.

- **Daytime Mode**: The clock uses a light silver/white background with dark hands and numbering to ensure maximum visibility in sunlight.
- **Nighttime Mode**: When your headlights activate (or when your dashboard switches to dark mode), the app will instantly switch to a **Pure Black** background. The clock hands and numbering shift to high-contrast white and dark green. This pure black helps preserve your night vision and matches the sleek interior styling of the cabin.

## 🚗 Driving Safety

This application has been certified as "Distraction Optimized" by Android Automotive. This means you do not have to worry about the car's safety system minimizing or blocking the clock while you are shifting into Drive or driving on the highway. The clock will remain visible on your dashboard as long as you leave the app open.
